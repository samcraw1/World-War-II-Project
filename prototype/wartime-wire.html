How to read the feed

Historical record. Documented events, written as plain wire copy. Dates and times are local to the event.

Reconstructed. Social-style posts from invented composite people. Their words are fiction; each one links to the records it is built on.

No real person is quoted or impersonated. Posts unlock only as you advance, so later events stay sealed.
